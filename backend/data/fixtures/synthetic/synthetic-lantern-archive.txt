The Lantern Archive is a fictional library on the imaginary island of Velmora. Its keepers record the colour of every lantern lit at dusk. Blue lanterns mark quiet evenings. Amber lanterns mark market days. Green lanterns are lit only when the tide clock chimes twice.

The archive has three reading rooms. The North Room stores tide clock diaries. The East Room stores lantern colour ledgers. The South Room is always closed for repainting, and nobody on Velmora remembers its original colour.
